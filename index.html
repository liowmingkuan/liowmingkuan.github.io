(04) Process

From brief to final, with AI in the right places.

  1. 01

    Concept

    Turn the brief into one clear idea and a visual direction the team can react to early.

    • ChatGPT
    • Gemini
    • Figma
  2. 02

    Storyboard

    Lock style, characters and environments in a frame-by-frame board, and get sign-off before production.

    • Nano Banana
    • Photoshop
    • Figma
  3. 03

    Production

    Generate and direct shots with consistent characters, or animate by hand when the craft calls for it.

    • Higgsfield
    • Seedance
    • After Effects
  4. 04

    Final

    Composite, edit and sound-design, then deliver platform-native cuts for every market and placement.

    • After Effects
    • Premiere Pro

(05) About

I'm Ming Kuan, a motion designer specialising in fintech marketing. I make complex products feel clear, confident and worth a second look.

For the past three years I've designed and animated paid campaigns across more than ten markets, from the UK and MENA to LATAM and APAC. I started in animation as a 2D artist at Lemon Sky Animation, contributing to The Last of Us Part II. That foundation in drawing, timing and storytelling now shapes how I direct AI tools: fast iteration, without losing art direction.
